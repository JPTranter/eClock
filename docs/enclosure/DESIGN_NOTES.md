# 3D Enclosure Design Notes

## Design Requirements
- Secure fit for XIAO nRF52840 board and ePaper display
- Accessible charging port
- Visible display window
- Mounting options (wall mount, desk stand)
- Battery compartment access

## Materials
- PLA or PETG for FDM printing
- Appropriate infill for durability
- Wall thickness considerations for battery safety

## Design Software
- OnShape (primary design tool)
- Export formats: STL, STEP
